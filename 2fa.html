Account security

Two-Factor Authentication

Add a second layer of protection to your account using an authenticator app on your phone.

Enable 2FA

  1. Log in with your email and password.
  2. Click the profile icon at the top right and choose Security Profile.
  3. Click Enable.
  4. Confirm your password and submit.
  5. You'll be redirected to a page showing a QR code and your recovery codes.
  6. Copy the recovery codes and keep them somewhere safe.
  7. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator (available on the Play Store and App Store).
  8. Your account is now added to the authenticator app.

Log in with 2FA

  1. Log out.
  2. Log back in with your email and password.
  3. When the 2FA form appears, enter the one-time password (OTP) shown in your authenticator app.
  4. Enter the code quickly — it expires after a short time.
  5. Alternatively, use one of your saved recovery codes.

Disable 2FA

  1. Log in with your email and password.
  2. Click the profile icon at the top right and choose Security Profile.
  3. Click Disable.
  4. Confirm your password.
  5. You'll be redirected and 2FA is now disabled.
Important: if you disable 2FA and enable it again, remove the old entry from your authenticator app and scan the new QR code.
